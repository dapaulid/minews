## Putin Wants to Achieve War Goals

[Neue Zürcher Zeitung](https://www.nzz.ch/international/us-friedensplan-fuer-die-ukraine-putin-will-kriegsziele-erreichen-ld.1913227) • _Tuesday, November 25, 2025, 05:30 AM_

The Kremlin presents the American peace plan as a consequence of the Alaska summit. The message is: It is suitable as a basis for negotiations as long as Russia gets what it demands.

### Spirit of Anchorage
- In mid-August, [Donald Trump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Trump) and [Vladimir Putin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Putin) met in [Alaska](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alaska), creating the «Spirit of Anchorage».
- Trump abandoned an immediate ceasefire and accepted Putin's condition: Weapons fall silent only after a comprehensive peace agreement.
- Russia sees consensus with the USA there, to which [Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukraine) and Europe must submit.

### US Peace Plan and Negotiations
- The 28-point plan is seen as acceptable as a basis for negotiations as a consequence of the summit, as long as Russia achieves its goals.
- Putin confirmed willingness to talk; the military situation in Ukraine pleases Moscow and fulfills the goals of the «special operation».
- [Yury Ushakov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yury_Ushakov) called the European counter-proposal unconstructive; discussions with the USA still pending.
- Plan was changed in US-Ukrainian negotiations; central points like army reduction, withdrawal from [Donbas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donbas) and NATO membership indispensable for Russia.

### Putin's Goals and Kremlin Perspective
- Putin wants to achieve war goals militarily or in negotiations, which means Kyiv's capitulation.
- Demands: Reduction of Ukrainian army, renunciation of [NATO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NATO) membership, end to Western dominance.
- Goal: Russia-friendly policy in Kyiv (internally towards Russian-speaking population and church, externally towards Moscow).
- Use the opportunity to improve relations with the USA, attract investments and displace America from Europe; [Kirill Dmitriev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kirill_Dmitriev) is negotiating.

### Russian Commentators and Public
- Anti-Western commentators hopeful: [Boris Mezhuyev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boris_Mezhuyev) sees freezing as a step towards the «Russian World»; [Alexander Dugin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Dugin) predicts full control over Ukraine in two years.
- Majority of Russians wish for a quick end to the war due to everyday burdens, not out of morality; Putin ignores this for now.
- Polls confirm willingness to make sacrifices; economy threatens recession, state collects money from citizens.

### Outlook
- After the end of the war: Return of traumatized soldiers, ongoing repression against «internal enemies».
- Society changed; return to normality before February 24, 2022 illusory.

> Relevance score: [8] - Geopolitical update on Russia-Ukraine war and US peace efforts; significant global security implications with potential long-term effects on Europe.

## Data Protector Warns Against US Clouds: Risks for Authorities

[Schweizer Radio und Fernsehen](https://www.srf.ch/news/schweiz/datenschutz-cloud-zuercher-datenschuetzerin-warnt-vor-us-clouds-fuer-behoerden) • _Tuesday, November 25, 2025, 05:51 AM_

More and more authorities are using US clouds. The Zurich Data Protection Officer warns of loss of control over sensitive data.

### Warning from the Data Protector
- The Data Protection Officer of the [Canton of Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canton_of_Zurich), [Dominika Blonski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominika_Blonski), warns of risks when using US [clouds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cloud_computing) like [Microsoft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft) or [Amazon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_Web_Services).
- More and more cantons and municipalities are using these IT offers without thorough examination of data protection risks.

### Changed Trend
- Previously great reluctance towards US offers.
- Today, institutions at cantonal and municipal level resort to them without prior consideration.

### Concrete Examples
- Microsoft programs in use in [Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canton_of_Zurich), [Bern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canton_of_Bern) and [Aargau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canton_of_Aargau).
- [Basel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canton_of_Basel-Stadt) recently decided, in [Lucerne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canton_of_Lucerne) it is being debated.
- Many municipalities are also using them.

### Data Protection Risks
- For non-sensitive data (e.g. names, addresses) unproblematic.
- For sensitive data problematic: Foreign providers guarantee no protection.
  - Since 2018, US law ([CLOUD Act](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CLOUD_Act)) allows access to stored data, e.g. Swiss tax data.
  - Authorities lose control over critical information.
  - Affected persons often learn nothing about the access and cannot defend themselves.
- Companies can unilaterally change contract terms.

### Recommendations for Secure Use
- Encrypt sensitive data independently in advance.
- Better: Store data more securely elsewhere.

> Relevance score: [6] - Swiss national issue on government data privacy risks from US cloud providers; civic relevance for governance and data sovereignty, but warning rather than crisis.

## Southeast Asia: Floods and Landslides Claim Victims

[Schweizer Radio und Fernsehen](https://www.srf.ch/news/international/wegen-heftigen-regenfaellen-suedostasien-ueberschwemmungen-und-erdrutsche-fordern-todesopfer) • _Monday, November 24, 2025, 9:29 PM_

Over 100 people have died, Vietnam particularly hard hit.

### Floods in Southeast Asia
- Heavy rains cause persistent [floods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flood) and [landslides](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landslide) in [Southeast Asia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southeast_Asia).
- Death toll rises to over 100; tens of thousands of people evacuated.
- Damage estimated at around 500 million dollars so far.

### Vietnam
- Particularly hard hit with at least 91 dead and 11 missing.
- Entire regions underwater, including provinces like [Khánh Hòa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khánh_Hòa_province) and [Đắk Lắk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Đắk_Lắk_province).
- Undermined roads destroyed; helicopters drop aid supplies and evacuate those affected.
- Weather authority warns of persistent rains and further risks.
- Vietnam is one of the countries most [threatened by floods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flood); [climate change](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Climate_change) intensifies extreme events.

### Thailand
- In the south, almost two million people affected; at least 5 dead and 4 injured.
- Floods in regions like [Songkhla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Songkhla_province); residents and tourists trapped in houses/hotels.
- Rescue boats deployed; authorities warn of rising water levels until Tuesday.

### Malaysia and Cambodia
- In Malaysia, over 12,500 evacuations in nine federal states; floods worsen due to persistent rains.
- [Cambodia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambodia) also affected with flooded regions.

> Relevance score: [6] - Natural disaster with 100+ deaths in SE Asia; humanitarian scale but regional, short-term impact.
