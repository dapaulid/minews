## It's All or Nothing Now: Blocher Wants to Split UBS

[Inside Paradeplatz](https://insideparadeplatz.ch/2025/11/23/jetzt-gehts-ums-ganze-blocher-will-ubs-aufspalten/) • _Sunday, November 23, 2025, 07:58 a.m._

Separate banking system, Swiss style: SVP tycoon proposes split into UBS Switzerland and UBS America. Media battle picks up speed.

### Blocher's Proposal for UBS Split
- The *SonntagsZeitung* presents the idea from SVP doyen [Christoph Blocher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christoph_Blocher), to split [UBS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UBS) into a UBS Switzerland and a UBS Overseas.
- Reason: UBS is “[too big to fail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Too_big_to_fail)” – “too big for Switzerland”; more regulations or equity do not solve the problem.

### Reactions at UBS
- Alarm sirens in the Zurich headquarters; for Chairman [Colm Kelleher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colm_Kelleher) and CEO [Sergio Ermotti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergio_Ermotti), it would be the end of power, size, and bonuses.
- Ermotti called the relocation debate on a [JPMorgan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JPMorgan_Chase) podium “bullshit.”
- Support for Kelleher in the *SonntagsBlick*: Clarification on [Financial Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_Times) story about “secret talks” with Trump's finance minister – no UBS relocation to New York, but an old customer relationship.

### Concrete Split Plan
- Give shareholders two stocks: one for UBS Switzerland (under Swiss law), one for UBS America (under US law).
- Complete separation into two independent banks with headquarters in two countries.
- Switzerland no longer liable for US business; UBS Switzerland possibly still subject to “too big to fail” review.

### Examples and Benefits
- Models: Cement multinational spun off US business as [Amrize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holcim); [Dottikon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dottikon_Exclusive_Synthesis) from [Ems-Chemie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EMS-Chemie) developed splendidly.
- Benefits for Switzerland, the bank, owners, and America.
- Blocher to Ermotti: “Death is part of life” – every company is threatened by death, but must not drag down the national economy.

> Relevance score: [7] - Proposal by influential figure to restructure major Swiss bank amid risk concerns; could affect national economy and banking stability if pursued, sparking significant debate.

## Pfister Wants to Buy Fewer US Fighter Jets for Now - the Sunday News

[Watson](https://www.watson.ch/international/schweiz/928798271-pfister-will-vorerst-weniger-us-kampfjets-kaufen-die-sonntagsnews) • _Sunday, November 23, 2025, 07:10 a.m._

Pfister wants fewer F-35 fighter jets, Epstein connections to Switzerland, and investigations in fast-track proceedings against serial thieves: The Sunday news.

### Pfister Wants to Buy Fewer US Jets – for Now
- Defense Minister [Martin Pfister](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Pfister_(Politiker)) plans, according to the «SonntagsBlick,» to buy 30 instead of 36 [F-35](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lockheed_Martin_F-35) fighter jets for now, to stay within the six billion franc cost ceiling.
- A later option for ten additional jets remains open; the federal council's decision is imminent.

### Epstein Also Had Contacts in Zurich and Zug
- Documents of the deceased sex offender [Jeffrey Epstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeffrey_Epstein) show connections to Switzerland, particularly for the alleged procurement of young women for his sex trafficking («NZZ am Sonntag»).
  - A 2016 email offered a “assistant from Zurich,” where “assistant” was a code word.
  - Epstein was a client of a Swiss bank; connections to [Zug](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canton_of_Zug) Crypto Valley existed.
- The Federal Office of Justice received no mutual legal assistance request from the US.

### Serial Offender from Maghreb: Authorities Testing New Fast-Track Proceedings
- Investigators are using fast-track proceedings (48 hours from arrest to penalty order) against serial offenders from North Africa («SonntagsZeitung»).
- The federal system favors delinquents through limited detention duration (max. two days), facilitating cantonal changes.
- Taskforce “Security in the Asylum and Foreign Nationals Sector” coordinates pilot project nationwide; Asylum Committee discusses extended detention options.

### Majority of Swiss Women and Men for Recognition of Palestine
- 57% of respondents support recognition of [Palestine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestinian_Authority) as a state (Sotomo survey, «NZZ am Sonntag», November 7–20, 2088 people in German- and Western Switzerland).
  - 33% oppose, 10% undecided.
  - Strong support among women (almost 2/3) and in Western Switzerland.

### Roche Boss Wasn't Rich Enough for Trump Meeting
- [Roche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roche_Holding) Chairman [Severin Schwan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Severin_Schwan) was not allowed to attend the meeting of Swiss corporations with [Donald Trump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Trump) because he wasn't rich enough («NZZ am Sonntag»).
- Schwan participated in video calls with [SECO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_Secretariat_for_Economic_Affairs) Director [Helene Budliger Artieda](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helene_Budliger_Artieda) and contacted corporate chiefs to increase direct investments.
- [UBS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UBS) Chairman [Colm Kelleher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colm_Kelleher) supported in the customs dispute via US contacts («SonntagsBlick»).

### SP Wants to Examine Joint Air Force with Austria
- The Swiss [SP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_Democratic_Party_of_Switzerland) is examining a joint air force with [Austria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austria) for cost savings and increased security (position paper, Tamedia).
- Rejection of [F-35](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lockheed_Martin_F-35) and [Patriot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIM-104_Patriot) system; light jets (about a dozen) better against drones, as Patriot shot costs 2 million francs.

### More Question Marks Around Migros Deal in Germany
- [Migros](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Migros) Zurich's stake in German retailer [Tegut](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tegut) carries higher risks: high rental and loan debts («SonntagsZeitung»).
  - Off-balance-sheet commitments >730 million euros; potential loss if abandoned: about 1 billion francs.
- Migros contradicts: Valuation ignores assets.

### Berset Staff Received Hundreds of Thousands of Francs in Severance
- After the resignation of former Federal Councillor [Alain Berset](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alain_Berset), staff received >750,000 francs in severance pay («SonntagsBlick»).
  - [Lukas Gresch](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lukas_Gresch): 370,000 francs.
  - Stefan Honegger: 150,000 francs.
  - Michael Brändle: slightly less.
  - Rémy Lüthy: 54,000 francs; Gianna Blum: 44,000 francs.
