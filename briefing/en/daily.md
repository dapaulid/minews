## US government reduces number of recommended vaccinations for children

[Watson](https://www.watson.ch/international/schweiz/564253425-us-regierung-senkt-zahl-der-fuer-kinder-empfohlenen-impfungen) • _Tuesday, January 6, 2026, 05:08_

The administration of President **[Donald Trump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Trump)** now advises far fewer vaccinations for children than before.

### Changes to vaccination recommendations in the US
- The government of President **[Donald Trump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Trump)** reduces the recommended childhood vaccinations from **17** to **11**.  
- Announcement was made via **Truth Social**; parents may still choose any vaccine, and costs remain covered by health insurers.  
- The new recommendations focus on the “eleven most serious and dangerous diseases.”  
- **Vaccines removed** (no longer generally recommended):  
  - Rotavirus  
  - Hepatitis A  
  - Hepatitis B  
  - RSV infections  
  - Meningococcal B  
  - Meningococcal ACWY  

### New US vaccination recommendations
- The following vaccinations are now recommended:  
  - Diphtheria  
  - Tetanus  
  - Pertussis (whooping cough)  
  - Haemophilus influenzae type b (Hib)  
  - Pneumococcal  
  - Polio  
  - Measles  
  - Mumps  
  - Rubella  
  - Human papillomavirus (**[HPV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_papillomavirus)**)  
  - Chickenpox (Varicella)  

### Comparison with Switzerland
- The Swiss **[Federal Office of Public Health (FOPH)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Office_of_Public_Health_(Switzerland))** also recommends **11** basic vaccinations for children, which are virtually identical to the new US list.  
- In addition, the FOPH recommends:  
  - Rotavirus vaccine (optional)  
  - Meningococcal vaccines (optional)  
  - Further vaccinations for risk groups (e.g., COVID‑19, tick‑borne encephalitis, influenza)  

### Criticism and reactions
- The **[American Academy of Pediatrics (AAP)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Pediatrics)** calls the shortened recommendation **“dangerous and unnecessary.”**  
- The AAP highlights differences with other countries (e.g., Denmark) and warns against transplanting foreign vaccine schedules onto the United States.

> Relevance score: [8] – A drastic cut in US vaccination recommendations could have massive consequences for public health, vaccine confidence, and potentially the global disease landscape. Switzerland’s adoption of the new scheme makes the impact nationally relevant.

## Strategic partnership, resources and many debts

[Neue Zürcher Zeitung](https://www.nzz.ch/pro/china-ist-der-groesste-geldgeber-venezuelas-ld.1918759) • _Tuesday, January 6, 2026, 05:30_

With the ouster of Nicolás Maduro by the United States, Chinese‑provided loans are back in focus. Western investors also have outstanding claims.

### Strategic partnership between China and Venezuela
- Since 2001 an official strategic partnership has existed between **[China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China)** and **[Venezuela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venezuela)**.  
- Under **[Hugo Chávez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugo_Ch%C3%A1vez)** (2001‑2002) the cooperation was expanded within the framework of “21st‑century socialism,” also involving Russia and Iran.  
- **[Nicolás Maduro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicol%C3%A1s_Maduro)** met in September 2023, shortly before his arrest, with China’s special envoy for Latin America **[Qiu Xiaoqi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qiu_Xiaoqi)** at the Miraflores Palace.  

### China’s goals: resources and influence
- China secures supplies of **soybeans**, **copper**, and especially **crude oil** from Latin America to guarantee its own resource needs.  
- Venezuela is China’s largest buyer of **[PDVSA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petroleos_de_Venezuela)** crude in South America, even though shipments represent only about **5 %** of China’s total sea‑dry bulk oil imports.  
- The Chinese tech giant **[Huawei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huawei)** is also active in Venezuela.  

### Loan volume and collateral
- According to the research institute **[AidData](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AidData)** (University of William & Mary), the pledged loan volume over two decades amounts to roughly **US $106 billion**.  
- **[Sebastian Horn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sebastian_Horn)** of the **[University of Hamburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hamburg)** estimates about **US $90 billion**; both figures mainly comprise loans secured by future oil exports.  
- Revenues from the resource trade were deposited into a trust account of a Chinese bank.  

### Lack of transparency and repayment status
- China usually grants loans through state banks but does not publish detailed outstanding balances.  
- Rating agencies such as **[S&P Global](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%26P_Global)** primarily analyse dollar or euro bonds held by Western creditors.  
- After the price collapse in 2014, Venezuela received a **grace period** from China.  
- **Brad Parks** (AidData) doubts that the remaining amount can be reliably quantified; U.S. sanctions have further reduced oil exports to China.  
- Horn expects that after all restructurings, **US $10‑20 billion** will still be outstanding – a relatively small sum by Chinese standards.  

### Western creditors and restructuring pressure
- Western investors demand restructuring of **≈ US $60 billion** of claims that have been in default since 2017.  
- Lifting U.S. sanctions would be a prerequisite for renegotiating these debts.  
- In recent weeks the price of Venezuelan bonds rose from **US $0.20** to **US $0.30** per dollar, as investors hope for a possible return of Maduro and the associated relief.  
- The U.S. White House could, in a restructuring scenario, exert pressure to treat Western creditors preferentially – a potential precedent that worries Beijing.  

### Outlook: challenges to debt repayment
- Venezuela’s oil exports generated **US $18.4 billion** gross revenue in 2024 according to **[OPEC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OPEC)**, only a fraction of the loan volume.  
- The deteriorated state of industry and limited production increases make rapid repayment difficult.  
- For China the risk remains low, while geopolitical tensions between the United States, Venezuela and China continue to shape financing conditions.

> Relevance score: [6] – The ouster of Maduro and the consequent re‑orientation of Chinese loans, together with Western claims, could affect the geopolitical and economic situation in Latin America and indirectly influence global commodity markets.

## 29 dead and 1,200 arrests during protests in Iran

[Blick](https://www.blick.ch/ausland/studierende-gehen-landesweit-auf-die-strasse-29-tote-und-1200-festnahmen-bei-protesten-im-iran-id21571485.html) • _Monday, January 5, 2026, 22:37_

The judiciary of the Islamic Republic is cracking down hard amid nationwide demonstrations. At least 29 people have died, and more than 1,200 have been detained.

### Casualties and arrests
- At least **34 people** have been killed during the ongoing protests, **including four children** and **two security‑force members**.  
- More than **1,200 individuals** have been detained, according to the U.S.–based human‑rights network **[HRANA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_rights_in_Iran)**.  

### Geographic spread of protests
- Demonstrations have been reported in **a minimum of 88 cities** across **27 of the 31 provinces** of the **[Islamic Republic of Iran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_Republic_of_Iran)**.  
- After violent crackdowns in rural areas, the unrest reached major metropolises such as **[Tehran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tehran)** and **[Mashhad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mashhad)**.  

### Origins and development
- The protests were triggered more than a week ago by a **sharp shock to the Iranian foreign‑exchange market**, prompting angry market traders in Tehran to take to the streets.  
- The economic grievance quickly morphed into a **broader political protest** that has been met with the usual harsh response from the state.  
- On Monday, the **Judiciary of the Islamic Republic** announced that it would act “without leniency” against demonstrators.  

### Student participation and university incidents
- Students have joined the protests at **at least 17 universities nationwide**.  
- At the **University of Birjand** (capital of South Khorasan Province), violent arrests occurred on campus on Monday evening, as reported by the campus newspaper **[Amirkabir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amirkabir_University_of_Technology)**.

> Relevance score: [6] – A massive crackdown on demonstrators in Iran is an important human‑rights issue and can affect international relations and regional stability.
