## Putin doesn't budge an inch from his demands

[Blick](https://www.blick.ch/ausland/witkoff-und-kushner-bei-putin-kreml-unterhaendler-postet-friedenstaube-id21480713.html) • _Tuesday, December 2, 2025, 11:53 PM_

High-ranking US representatives discussed a peace plan for Ukraine in Moscow. After a long meeting with Putin, positive signals emerge. But the dispute over Russian territorial claims is hard to resolve.

### Meeting in Moscow
- High-ranking US delegation, including [Steve Witkoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Witkoff) (68) and [Jared Kushner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jared_Kushner) (44, son-in-law of [Donald Trump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Trump) (79)), discussed with [Vladimir Putin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Putin) (73) for almost five hours about the US peace plan for [Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukraine).
- Kremlin advisor [Yuri Ushakov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuri_Ushakov) (78): No progress, neither closer nor further from peace; much work needed, especially on Russian territorial claims.

### Russian Position
- [Putin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Putin) does not deviate from demands, requires cession of the entire [Donbas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donbas) in eastern Ukraine, although the Russian army does not control all parts.
- Kremlin negotiator [Kirill Dmitriev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kirill_Dmitriev) (50) described negotiations on X as «productive» and posted a peace dove emoji.

### Ukrainian Position
- [Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukraine) strictly rejects territorial cessions, as [Volodymyr Zelenskyy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volodymyr_Zelenskyy) (47) emphasized.
- Ukrainian delegation discussed for 6.5 hours in Florida over the weekend solely about Russian territorial demands.

### Peace Plan and Outlook
- US plan with originally 28 points (made public end of November), recently adjusted because parts were unacceptable for Ukraine and Europe; USA and Ukraine worked on it bilaterally.
- Witkoff is to discuss results with Trump.
- War, started on February 24, 2022 by Russian attack, continues with full force despite peace talks.

> Relevance score: [8] - US-Russia talks on Ukraine peace stalled on territorial demands; significant for ongoing global conflict with long-term geopolitical and European stability implications.

## Criticism of SBB: Billions contract to Siemens meets broad rejection

[20 Minuten](https://www.20min.ch/story/exklusive-umfrage-kritik-an-sbb-zwei-drittel-lehnen-mega-auftrag-an-siemens-ab-103462220) • _Wednesday, December 3, 2025, 6:01 AM_

The SBB's decision to award a billions contract to Siemens is met with massive criticism. Two thirds of the population are against it.

### Contract Award
- The [SBB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss_Federal_Railways) awarded a contract worth around 2 billion francs for 116 double-decker trains for Zurich and western Switzerland to [Siemens Mobility](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siemens_Mobility), not to the Swiss [Stadler Rail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stadler_Rail) of [Peter Spuhler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Spuhler).
- SBB's reasoning: Siemens offered the most advantageous bid according to legal requirements, with a price difference in the high three-digit millions over the service life; «Swissness» is not allowed under procurement law.
- SBB highlights: 6000 employees in Switzerland, purchases of over 550 million francs from 1900 Swiss companies.

### Poll Results
- Exclusive poll by Leewas for 20 Minuten and Tamedia: 65% (two thirds) of the population reject the award to Siemens.
- Rejection across all political camps, strongest skepticism among SVP supporters; less pronounced on the left side.

### Statements from Involved Parties
- [Stadler Rail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stadler_Rail): No comment due to ongoing appeal at the [Federal Administrative Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Administrative_Court_(Switzerland)).
- [Siemens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siemens): Attractive, competitive offer; train optimal for metropolitan areas with high comfort.

### Political Criticism
- [Gerhard Pfister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gerhard_Pfister) (Center): Wrong decision, SBB management must take political responsibility for taxpayers' money and acceptance; use rules without changing them.
- [David Roth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Roth_(politician)) (SP): Exhaust ecological and social leeway in tenders to secure jobs, within international rules.
- [Christian Imark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_Imark) (SVP): Disappointed, but question is procurement law-related; courts and Business Audit Committee (GPK) check compliance with rules.

### Aftermath
- Stadler's appeal at the Federal Administrative Court; possible review by GPK and further instances.

> Relevance score: [7] - Swiss national railway SBB awards 2B CHF contract to foreign Siemens over local firm amid public backlash; affects transport infrastructure, economy, and jobs in Switzerland.

## War crimes in the Caribbean? - Pete Hegseth under suspicion

[Neue Zürcher Zeitung](https://www.nzz.ch/international/kriegsverbrechen-in-der-karibik-pete-hegseth-steht-unter-verdacht-ld.1914632) • _Wednesday, December 3, 2025, 5:30 AM_

The deadly attacks on drug smugglers' boats in the Caribbean were already legally controversial. But now the US Secretary of Defense is suspected of also ordering the killing of shipwreck survivors. Even Republicans fear violations of the laws of war.

### Military Operations in the Caribbean
- Since September, the US military has destroyed over 21 boats of alleged [drug smugglers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drug_smuggling) in the Caribbean and eastern Pacific, killing 82 people.
- Many experts and allies like [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France) or [United Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Kingdom) consider the attacks illegal extrajudicial killings.

### Allegations against Pete Hegseth
- A [Washington Post](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Washington_Post) report claims: Before the first attack on September 2, US Secretary of Defense [Pete Hegseth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pete_Hegseth) allegedly gave the order «kill them all».
- Two survivors clinging to debris were allegedly killed by another missile, ordered by the commander of [SEAL Team 6](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Navy_SEALs).

### Concern among Republicans
- Republican congressmen express criticism:
  - [Mike Turner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Turner): «Illegal action», if true.
  - [Don Bacon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don_Bacon): Violation of [laws of war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_of_war).
  - Senate Armed Services Committee Chairman [Roger Wicker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roger_Wicker) demands «rigorous investigation» with access to video and audio recordings.

### Government Reactions
- Hegseth calls the report «lies» and shares a meme on [X](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/X_(social_network)) of a turtle fighting drug smugglers.
- [White House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_House): Admiral [Frank Bradley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_Bradley_(admiral)) acted within his authority, authorized by Hegseth.
- [Donald Trump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Trump): Would not have wanted a second attack; Hegseth denies the order.
- [New York Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_New_York_Times): No oral instruction; written directives do not mention survivors; Hegseth followed mission live, gave no further orders.

### Legal and Strategic Aspects
- Government employees justify: Boat and drugs remained legitimate target; military lawyer [Geoffrey Corn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geoffrey_Corn) considers attacks illegal, as no armed conflict.
- Trump administration sees «war on drug terrorists», justifies plans against [Nicolás Maduro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicol%C3%A1s_Maduro) in [Venezuela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venezuela); Justice Department memo classified.
- Hegseth's stance: Conduct wars «by our rules»; [laws of war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_of_war) «written so we lose»; demands «risk-taking and aggressive leaders».

> Relevance score: [6] - Allegations of war crimes against US Defense Secretary in Caribbean anti-drug operations killing 82; raises international law concerns but unproven and niche to military policy.
