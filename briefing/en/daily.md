## Inheritance Tax and Citizen Service: Votes Live

[Blick](https://www.blick.ch/politik/abstimmung-zur-erbschaftssteuer-und-service-citoyen-initiative-kann-die-juso-ein-historisches-debakel-verhindern-id21470164.html) • _Sunday, November 30, 2025, 12:10 AM_

Switzerland is voting on the Juso initiative for an inheritance tax and a new citizen service. Blick reports on all results, reactions, and assessments.

### Vote Results
- The [Juso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JUSO_(Switzerland))-Initiative for an [inheritance tax](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inheritance_tax) was rejected by three quarters of the Swiss, no canton voted in favor.
- The initiative for a new [citizen service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss_civilian_service) for young women and men failed with 84.2% no votes, also no canton in favor.

### Reactions to the Inheritance Tax Initiative
- [Mirjam Hostetmann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mirjam_Hostetmann) (Juso President): Opponents had a tenfold budget, result not surprising; applause for four years of youth work, the problem remains.
- [Katharina Prelicz-Huber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katharina_Prelicz-Huber) ([Greens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_Party_of_Switzerland)): Announces motion for moderate tax (5% on allowances over 5 million francs); criticism of opponents who called the initiative too extreme.
- Opponents in [Blick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blick_(Swiss_magazine))-roundtable:
  - [Marcel Dettling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcel_Dettling) ([SVP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss_People%27s_Party) President): Initiative "buried", Juso/SP have harmed themselves.
  - [Benjamin Mühlemann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benjamin_M%C3%BChlemann) ([FDP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FDP.The_Liberals) Co-President): Off the table for now, population tired of "envy debate".

### Reactions to the Citizen Service Initiative
- [Noémie Roten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No%C3%A9mie_Roten): Disappointed, but proud of public relations work as a small organization; voting complaint filed, focus was too much on costs instead of benefits; debate must continue, security not guaranteed.
- Opponents:
  - [Philipp Matthias Bregy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philipp_Matthias_Bregy) ([The Centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Centre_(political_party)) President): Pleased with no vote, criticizes as discriminatory; more women in the army voluntarily.
  - [Martin Pfister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Pfister) (Defense Minister): Campaigned for no, no rejection of voluntary engagement that keeps Switzerland alive.

> Relevance score: [9] - National referendums on inheritance tax and citizen service directly affect Swiss fiscal policy, governance, and civic rights; high structural relevance for population.

## Customs Dispute: Federal Council Misses Trump Deal and Loses Billions

[20 Minuten](https://www.20min.ch/story/zollstreit-mit-den-usa-streit-um-verpassten-trump-deal-verspielte-bundesrat-milliarden-103460628) • _Sunday, November 30, 2025, 2:18 AM_

The Federal Council is under criticism for allegedly missing a lucrative customs deal with Trump. Entrepreneurs warn of high losses.

### New Accusations Against the Federal Council
- Entrepreneur [[Alfred Gantner]] (co-founder of [[Partners Group]]) and [[SVP]] National Councilor [[Franz Grüter]] accuse the [[Federal Council (Switzerland)|Federal Council]] of missing a better [[customs dispute]] deal with [[Donald Trump]] in May.
  - Deal would have limited punitive tariffs to only 10 percent, instead of the current 39 percent.
- As a result, Swiss companies have paid around 1.5 billion francs in punitive tariffs, tech exports to the USA down 14 percent, 3000 jobs lost.

### Missed «Window of Opportunity»
- State Secretary [[Helene Budliger]] is said to have negotiated a largely finished draft in the spring.
- Delay by Federal Council: Weeks-long approval, internal co-reports, and coordination with [[EU]] agreements (e.g. food and health agreement).
- USA has since concluded more lucrative deals with other states; [[Ursula von der Leyen]] made concessions to Trump.

### Criticism of Departments and Counterpositions
- In focus: Departments of [[Ignazio Cassis]], [[Beat Jans]], and agricultural office – slowed down out of consideration for [[EU]].
  - Jans: «Untruths», Federal Council acted in the national interest.
  - Cassis’ department rejects accusations.
- Gantner criticizes [[Bilaterals III]] as an obstacle to US deal.

### Economic Consequences and Investigations
- [[ETH Zurich economists|ETH economists]] warn: Prolonged 39 percent tariffs could reduce GDP by up to 3.5 billion francs annually.
- Delegation of business leaders met Trump in November with 100,000-franc gifts; [[The Centre (Switzerland)|The Centre]] President [[Philipp Matthias Bregy]] demands clarification.
- Council of States Business Audit Commission has been investigating crisis organization and negotiations since August.
- Reduction to 15 percent «in the next few days» expected, exact date unclear.

> Relevance score: [7] - Alleged missed tariff deal with US could cost Swiss economy billions, impacting businesses and trade policy; significant national economic governance issue if substantiated.
