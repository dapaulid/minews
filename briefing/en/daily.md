## Donbas, NATO, Security: The Risks in Ukraine

[20 Min](https://www.20min.ch/story/treffen-mit-trump-das-sind-die-knacknuesse-in-selenskis-20-punkte-plan-fuer-frieden-103476478) • _Sunday, 28 December 2025, 04:42_

Ukraine President **[Volodymyr Zelenskyy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volodymyr_Zelenskyy)** travels to Florida to meet **U.S. President **[Donald Trump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Trump)**.  
- **Purpose:** Presentation of a revised **20‑point peace plan** that was already published on 24 December.  
- The meeting is seen as a ** rapprochement** between the United States and Ukraine, because Trump had previously agreed to a personal conversation only if a deal seemed possible.  

### Contents of Zelenskyy’s 20‑point plan
- **Ceasefire:** Immediate effect once all parties sign the plan.  
- **Trade and reconstruction package:** Closely aligned with U.S. interests, increasing the likelihood of Trump’s approval.  
- **Demilitarised zone in the Donbas:** Ukraine withdraws troops, Russia must vacate an equally sized area.  
- **Security guarantees:** Reference to guarantees similar to **Article 5 of the [NATO Treaty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Article_5_(NATO))**, but without a direct NATO accession.  
- **Nuclear plant [Zaporizhzhia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaporizhzhia_Nuclear_Power_Plant):** Proposal for joint U.S.–Ukrainian operation, while Zelenskyy rejects Russian involvement.  

### Factors that favour a quick peace
- **U.S.–Ukraine alignment** on several core points (ceasefire, reconstruction, trade agreement).  
- **Clear mechanism** for an immediate ceasefire raises implementation chances.  
- **Ukraine’s concession** regarding NATO membership acts as a significant compromise.  

### Obstacles to implementation
- **Russian demands in the Donbas:** President **[Vladimir Putin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Putin)** has so far refused any sharing of control; full Russian dominance remains his maximal goal.  
- **Security guarantees:** For Moscow, “Article‑5‑like” guarantees are a red line; Kyiv, however, deems them indispensable.  
- **Trump’s consent:** Trump continues to stress that nothing will be decided without his “green light”; he has already signalled disagreements.  
- **Nuclear controversy:** Different ideas on the management of the **Zaporizhzhia nuclear power plant** prevent a joint approach.  

### Current Russian position and events
- The Russian Foreign Ministry reports **“slow but steady progress”** in the negotiation process with the United States, but hardly mentions Ukraine.  
- **Putin** has not given up any of his maximal demands; experts see few signs of change.  
- Shortly before Trump’s meeting, Russia carried out **large‑scale missile attacks** on Ukraine, including on **Kyiv**, accusing Kyiv of “torpedoing” the peace talks.  

---  

*The plan offers avenues for peace, but central obstacles – especially Russia’s positions on the Donbas and security guarantees – remain decisive for the outcome of the negotiations.*

> Relevance score: [8] – Ukraine peace plan touches on security, NATO and European stability; outcomes could affect many countries and alter the geopolitical landscape.

## Kidney Research Revolutionizes Dementia Prevention

[AD HOC NEWS](https://www.ad-hoc-news.de/boerse/news/ueberblick/nieren-forschung-revolutioniert-demenz-vorbeugung/68436510) • _Saturday, 27 December 2025, 18:55_

New studies show that reduced kidney function accelerates neurodegenerative processes and raises the risk of **[dementia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dementia)**. Simple urine tests could later serve as an early‑warning system.

### Key findings  
- Several new studies confirm the **“kidney‑brain axis”**: impaired kidney function speeds up neurodegeneration and increases the risk for **[dementia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dementia)**.  
- In patients at the end stage of **[chronic kidney disease (CKD)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chronic_kidney_disease)**, transcranial sonography revealed structural changes in the **[substantia nigra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Substantia_nigra)**.  

### Pathophysiological mechanisms  
- **Toxin flood:**  
  - When kidneys fail, neurotoxic waste products are no longer filtered.  
  **Uremic toxins** enter the brain, accelerating neurodegenerative processes.  
- **Restless‑leg syndrome:**  
  - Common in dialysis patients; highlights the close link between renal and cerebral pathology.  
- **Neuronal adaptation:**  
  - Research by the **[German Center for Neurodegenerative Diseases (DZNE)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deutsches_Zentrum_f%C3%BCr_Neurodegenerative_Erkrankungen)** shows that uremic toxins block the fine structural adaptations of neurons needed for learning and memory.  

### Risk groups  
- **Sex‑specific differences:**  
  - According to an analysis by **[Marshall University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marshall_University)**, men with CKD have a significantly higher risk of cognitive decline than women.  
  - In men, kidney impairment more rapidly worsens cardiac output, reducing cerebral perfusion – a vicious cycle between heart, kidney and brain.  

### Practical implications  
- **Interdisciplinary collaboration:** Neurologists and **[nephrologists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nephrology)** should work closely together to enable early diagnosis.  
- **Early‑warning urine test:**  
  - Routine measurement of **[albumin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albumin)** in urine can serve as a simple indicator of increased dementia risk.  
- **Lifestyle adjustments:**  
  - Unbalanced **[electrolyte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrolytes)** levels (e.g., caused by extreme “hydration hacks”) can jeopardise neuronal stability in pre‑existing kidney damage.  
- **Outlook:** Clinical trials starting in 2026 will test whether kidney‑protective drugs also slow cognitive decline.  

### Recommendations for those affected  
- **Prioritise kidney health:** Regular check‑ups, a healthy diet and avoidance of nephrotoxic exposures.  
- **Cardiovascular support (for men):** Measures that boost heart performance can indirectly lower dementia risk.  
- **Cognitive exercises:** Combine physical kidney‑protective strategies with daily memory and concentration drills (e.g., from the free report “Brain Training Made Easy”).  

> Relevance score: [7] – Potentially groundbreaking medical insight that could change early‑detection and prevention of dementia globally, with long‑term health and economic implications.

## Strong Earthquake Shakes Taiwan

[Neue Zürcher Zeitung](https://www.nzz.ch/panorama/starkes-erdbeben-erschuettert-taiwan-ld.1918153) • _Saturday, 27 December 2025, 20:45_

Taiwan: Magnitude 7.0 quake causes nationwide shaking

### Event
- A **earthquake** of magnitude **7.0** on the [Richter magnitude scale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richter_magnitude_scale) struck Taiwan late Saturday night (local time).  
- The epicentre was offshore, about **32 km** from the coastal city of **Yilan** ([Yilan County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yilan_County)).  

### Impacts
- The quake was felt across the entire island, including the capital **Taipei** ([Taipei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taipei)).  
- **Power outages:**  
  - More than **3 000 households** in Yilan were briefly without electricity, reported by the **Taiwan Power Company** ([Taiwan Power Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiwan_Power_Company)).  
- **Property damage:**  
  - The national fire authority is assessing the damage; the Taipei city administration has so far reported no major damage.  
- **Operational disruptions:**  
  - The world’s largest chip‑fabricator **TSMC** ([TSMC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TSMC)) had to evacuate some facilities temporarily; staff have already returned to their workstations.  

### Response and measures
- **Evacuations:** TSMC organised short‑term evacuations and then restored normal operations.  
- **Power supply:** Taiwan Power Company is working on a rapid restoration of electricity in the affected areas.  
- **Damage assessment:** Ongoing investigations by the national fire authority and local officials.  

### Seismic risk and history
- **Tectonic setting:** Taiwan sits at the junction of two [tectonic plates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tectonic_plate), making it highly earthquake‑prone.  
- **Past events:**  
  - **2016:** A southern Taiwan quake killed over **100 people** ([2016 Taiwan earthquake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Taiwan_earthquake)).  
  - **1999:** A magnitude **7.3** quake caused more than **2 000 deaths** ([1999 Jiji earthquake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1999_Jiji_earthquake)).  

> Relevance score: [6] – A magnitude‑7 earthquake causes significant local damage and may affect regional supply chains, yet the direct impact on Switzerland and the world is limited.

## “We’re Hiring” – Migros President Aims to Create 1,000 New Jobs Annually by 2029

[blue News](https://www.bluewin.ch/de/news/schweiz/migros-praesidentin-will-bis-2029-jaehrlich-1000-neue-arbeitsplaetze-schaffen-3025573.html) • _Saturday, 27 December 2025, 18:40_

Migros plans a major job‑creation drive in the coming years. President **Ursula Nold** announces new stores – and a large‑scale expansion of positions.

### Planned expansion  

- **Ursula Nold**, President of **[Migros](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Migros)**, has announced the opening of **140 new stores** by 2029.  
- Goal: **1 000 new jobs per year**.  
- The expansion will be the **largest** in the company’s history and is driven mainly by changing shopping habits:  
  - Customers shop more frequently and purposefully, often after work.  
  - Smaller stores aim to reach about **200 000 additional households** more effectively.  

### New store concept  

- Focus on **smaller, streamlined shops** (“lean neighbour store”).  
- Example: The newly opened store in **[Spreitenbach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spreitenbach)** (canton Aargau) now has only **one staffed checkout**, while staff numbers remain unchanged.  
- No full automation:  
  - Employees are expected to concentrate more on **customer advice**, **freshness** and **goods management**.  

### Labour market: job cuts and creation  

- Recent years saw a **large‑scale job reduction**:  
  – Sale of business units (e.g., **[Hotelplan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hotelplan)**, **[Mibelle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mibelle)**, **Melectronics**, **SportX**) led to the loss of **up to 1 500 full‑time positions**.  
  – Mid‑2024, about **150 jobs** were cut at the Zürich headquarters.  
  – Closure of four outlets (Eglisau, Niederurnen, Aathal, Bachenbülach) by early 2026 will affect **24 employees** (re‑allocation offers to other supermarket positions are provided).  
- **Reallocation:**  
  – Of roughly **1 300 affected employees**, about **800** found a new position within Migros.  
- **New positions:**  
  – Currently there are **about 1 000 open jobs** and **850 apprenticeships**.  
  – The store expansion should add **1 000 additional employees each year**.  

### Background and context  

- The expansion is meant to mark a **turning point** after recent restructurings.  
- The aim is to increase **customer proximity** while also **creating employment opportunities**, without planning further **lay‑offs**.  

> Relevance score: [6] – Large retailer’s job‑creation plan will affect Swiss employment and regional economies; significant nationally but not a structural shift for the whole country.
