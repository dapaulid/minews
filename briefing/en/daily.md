## Now It's All or Nothing: Blocher Wants to Split UBS

[Inside Paradeplatz](https://insideparadeplatz.ch/2025/11/23/jetzt-gehts-ums-ganze-blocher-will-ubs-aufspalten/) • _Sunday, November 23, 2025, 07:58_

Separate Banking System, Swiss Style: SVP tycoon proposes split into UBS Switzerland and UBS America. Media battle picks up speed.

### Blocher's Split Proposal for UBS
- The *SonntagsZeitung* presents the idea of [SVP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss_People%27s_Party) doyen [Christoph Blocher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christoph_Blocher): Split the [UBS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UBS) into a UBS Switzerland and a UBS Overseas (America) to solve the “[too big to fail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Too_big_to_fail)” problem.
- Blocher: UBS is “too big for Switzerland,” more regulation or equity won't help; Switzerland is bursting the Alpine republic through UBS risks.

### Reactions from UBS Leadership
- For Chairman [Colm Kelleher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colm_Kelleher) and CEO [Sergio Ermotti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergio_Ermotti), the split would be the end of power, size, and bonuses.
- Ermotti calls relocation debate “bullshit” on [JPMorgan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JPMorgan_Chase) podium.
- *SonntagsBlick* (Ringier) provides support for Kelleher: [Financial Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_Times) story about “secret talks” with Trump's finance minister was not about UBS relocation to New York, but about old customer relationship.

### Details of the Feasible Plan
- Give shareholders two stocks:
  - One for UBS Switzerland under Swiss law (headquarters Zurich).
  - One for UBS America under US law (no additional equity needed if USA does not require it).
- Complete separation into two independent banks with headquarters in two countries; Switzerland no longer liable for US business.
- If UBS Switzerland alone is still “too big to fail,” solve separately.

### Examples and Benefits
- Models:
  * [Holcim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holcim) spun off US business as [Amrize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holcim#LafargeHolcim).
  * [Dottikon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dottikon_ES) separated from [Ems-Chemie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ems-Chemie) (Blocher family) and developed splendidly.
- Blocher: Beneficial for Switzerland, the bank, owners, and America; resistance from Ermotti understandable, but inevitable like “death” – a fundamentally healthy company must not drag down the national economy.

> Relevance score: [7] - Proposal by influential Swiss figure to restructure UBS amid risk concerns post-Credit Suisse; significant for Swiss financial stability and economy, sparking national debate.

## Republika Srpska: Moscow Ally Lobbies in the USA

[Schweizer Radio und Fernsehen](https://www.srf.ch/news/international/nationalistischer-politiker-republika-srpska-moskau-verbuendeter-lobbyiert-in-den-usa) • _Sunday, November 23, 2025, 10:09_

Milorad Dodik is a controversial politician in the Balkans. Why have the USA lifted sanctions against him?

### Milorad Dodik and His Role
- [Milorad Dodik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milorad_Dodik), controversial politician in the [Balkans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balkans), has dominated the [Republika Srpska](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republika_Srpska), the Serb-dominated part of [Bosnia-Herzegovina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bosnia_and_Herzegovina), for almost two decades.
- Represents nationalist policy with repeated secession threats that harbor war risks; close ties to [Vladimir Putin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Putin).
- [Barack Obama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barack_Obama) imposed sanctions in 2017 for destabilization, later extended to his circle; sanctions lifted at the end of October 2025, despite Dodik's unchanged stance.

### Recent Developments
- Court in [Sarajevo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarajevo) imposed a six-year office ban for violation of the [peace agreement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dayton_Agreement), which ended the [Bosnian War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bosnian_War) in 1995.
- Dodik stepped down as President of Republika Srpska; successor to be elected on Sunday, but he will likely remain influential in the background.

### Suspected Deal and Lobbying
- [Kurt Bassuener](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratization_Policy_Council), co-director of the think tank [Democratization Policy Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratization_Policy_Council) in Sarajevo, suspects an agreement: Dodik accepts the verdict and formally steps back, USA lifts sanctions.
- Public US lobbying documents prove million-dollar contracts with persons and firms, targeted at US president; previous lobbying work now escalates.

### Spread of Conspiracy Theories
- Dodik stages himself as a victim of the global elite, analogous to [Trump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Trump), allegedly fighting against Muslims and globalists who oppress Christians.
  - Opinion piece in the [Washington Post](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Washington_Post) by [Rod Blagojevich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rod_Blagojevich), ex-governor of Illinois, pardoned by Trump, with lobbying contract from Republika Srpska: Sarajevo as Iran proxy.
  - Tweet by [Laura Loomer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laura_Loomer), MAGA influencer.
  - Podcast in MAGA sphere: Dodik claims Bosnia will become a Muslim state, justice misused.
- Reality: No Islamist tendencies in Bosnia-Herzegovina; Dodik convicted on legal basis, has de facto accepted the verdict.

### Official Reaction and Analysis
- US State Department (on CNN request): No connection to lobbying, but reaction to constructive steps by the Parliament of Republika Srpska.
- Observers see the sudden lifting of all sanctions (not gradual) as clear indication of successful US lobbying.

> Relevance score: [6] - Geopolitical shift with US lifting sanctions on Bosnian Serb leader Dodik, Russia ally, potentially destabilizing Balkans peace; moderate global relevance to European security but limited direct Swiss impact.

## Pfister Wants to Buy Fewer US Fighter Jets for Now - the Sunday News

[Watson](https://www.watson.ch/international/schweiz/928798271-pfister-will-vorerst-weniger-us-kampfjets-kaufen-die-sonntagsnews) • _Sunday, November 23, 2025, 07:10_

Pfister wants fewer F-35 fighter jets, Epstein connections to Switzerland, and fast-track investigations against serial thieves: The Sunday news.

### Pfister Wants to Buy Fewer US Jets for Now
- Defense Minister [Martin Pfister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Pfister_(politician)) plans, according to «SonntagsBlick», to purchase 30 instead of 36 [F-35](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lockheed_Martin_F-35) fighter jets to stay within the six billion franc cost ceiling.
- Option for ten additional jets in a later phase remains open.
- Decision by the federal government is imminent.

### Epstein Connections to Switzerland
- Documents show contacts of the deceased sex offender [Jeffrey Epstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeffrey_Epstein) to Zurich and Zug, including alleged procurement of young women for sex trafficking.
  - 2016 email offered “assistant from Zurich”; “assistant” as code word in Epstein circle.
- Epstein was a customer of a Swiss bank; connections to [Zug Crypto Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crypto_Valley).
- No mutual legal assistance request from the [Federal Office of Justice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Office_of_Justice) from the USA.

### Fast-Track Proceedings Against Serial Offenders from the Maghreb
- Investigators are examining 48-hour fast-track proceedings (arrest to penalty order) against serial thieves from North Africa, reports «SonntagsZeitung».
- Federal system favors delinquents: Max. two days detention per canton.
- Taskforce “Security in Asylum and Foreign Nationals Sector” coordinates pilot project nationwide.
- Asylum Committee discusses expanded detention possibilities.

### Majority for Recognition of Palestine
- [Sotomo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sotomo) poll for «NZZ am Sonntag»: 57% of Swiss support recognition of [Palestine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_of_Palestine) as a state (2088 respondents, Nov. 7–20).
  - 33% against, 10% undecided.
  - Strong support among women (almost 2/3) and in western Switzerland.

### Roche Boss Too Poor for Trump Meeting
- [Roche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roche_Holding) Chairman [Severin Schwan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Severin_Schwan) was not allowed to attend the meeting of Swiss corporations with [Donald Trump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Trump) because not rich enough, according to «Team Switzerland» under Seco Director [Helene Budliger Artieda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helene_Budliger_Artieda).
- Schwan active in the background via video calls and contacts to corporate chiefs.
- [UBS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UBS) Chairman [Colm Kelleher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colm_Kelleher) supports Switzerland in tariff dispute with USA.

### SP for Joint Air Force with Austria
- [SP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_Democratic_Party_of_Switzerland) wants to examine alternative defense concept: Joint air force with [Austria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austria) for cost savings and more security (position paper, Tamedia).
- Rejection of [F-35](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lockheed_Martin_F-35) and [Patriot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIM-104_Patriot) system; shot costs two million francs, unsuitable against drones.
- Proposal: Purchase of about a dozen light jets.

### More Risks in Migros-Tegut Deal
- [Migros Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Migros) sees higher risks in participation in German retailer [Tegut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tegut): High rental and credit obligations (>730 million euros off-balance-sheet).
- Potential loss of around one billion francs if abandoned, contradicts Migros (ignores assets), reports «SonntagsZeitung».

### Severance Payments for Berset Staff
- After resignation of former [Federal Councillor Alain Berset](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alain_Berset), staff received >750,000 francs in severance («SonntagsBlick»).
  - Highest: Chief of Staff [Lukas Gresch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lukas_Gresch) with 370,000 francs.
  - [Stefan Honegger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stefan_Honegger): 150,000 francs.
  - Others: Michael Brändle (~150,000), Rémy Lüthy (54,000), Gianna Blum (44,000).

> Relevance score: [6] - Swiss defense minister scales back F-35 purchases; affects national security budget and procurement, moderate civic relevance in Switzerland.
