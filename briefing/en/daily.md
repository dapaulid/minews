## Sudan: UN warns of new cycle of violence in civil war

[20 Min](https://www.20min.ch/story/groesste-humanitaere-krise-sudan-buergerkrieg-fordert-dringend-internationale-aufmerksamkeit-103475792) • _Thursday, 25 December 2025, 03:27_

The humanitarian crisis in Sudan is escalating. Millions are affected, while international aid is urgently needed.

### Background  
- A civil war between the Sudanese army and the militia **[Rapid Support Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rapid_Support_Forces)** has been ongoing since April 2023.  
- The conflict threatens half of the roughly 50 million inhabitants of **[Sudan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sudan)** with hunger.  

### Humanitarian Situation  
- The United Nations describes the war as the world’s biggest humanitarian crisis.  
- **12 million** people have been displaced; every second citizen is threatened by food scarcity.  
- The city **[Al‑Fashir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Fashir)** has been plagued by hunger for months, and aid organisations have no access.  
- In the southeastern **[Kordofan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kordofan)** region, attacks have already occurred on:  
  - a kindergarten,  
  - hospitals,  
  - World Food Programme trucks.  

### Political Demands  
- German development minister **Reem Alabali Radovan** demands:  
  - immediate, larger international support,  
  - a stable ceasefire as a starting point for a political solution,  
  - that Sudan does not fall into oblivion.  

### UN Warnings  
- **[Tom Fletcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Fletcher_(humanitarian))**, UN emergency relief coordinator, warns of a new “cycle of violence” and a potential epicenter of violence in Kordofan.  
- Falling aid funds force the UN team daily to make life‑and‑death decisions, such as cutting project funding that can immediately cause people to die.
