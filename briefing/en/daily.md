## China launches new military exercise around Taiwan

[Watson](https://www.watson.ch/international/china/890732291-china-beginnt-neue-militaeruebung-rund-um-taiwan) • _Monday, December 29 2026, 03:39 a.m._

China has again begun a large‑scale military exercise around Taiwan.

### Background and start of the exercise
- The People’s Republic **[China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China)** has launched the massive manoeuvre **“Mission Justice 2025”**, focusing on air and sea zones around **[Taiwan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiwan)**.  
- The announcement comes from the state news agency **[Xinhua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xinhua_News)**, which quoted the Chinese military.  

### Details of the military manoeuvres
- Objectives of the drills:
  - Patrols ready for combat  
  - Achieving comprehensive superiority  
  - Blockading key ports and areas  
- A spokesperson called the actions a “serious warning” to “separatist” forces and a “legitimate and necessary” measure to safeguard Chinese sovereignty and national unity.  
- Additional announcements:
  - Live‑fire exercises starting Tuesday in **five designated zones** around Taiwan  
    - Uninvolved ships and aircraft are to avoid these zones (listed in sub‑points).  

### Reactions and geopolitical tensions
- The move comes after heightened tension with the **[United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States)**, which has announced a substantial weapons package for Taiwan.  
- These are the first large Chinese drills near Taiwan since April.  
- The People’s Liberation Army (**[PLA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People%27s_Liberation_Army)**) regularly conducts manoeuvres in the region with warships and military aircraft.  

### Taiwan’s response
- Taiwan sharply condemned the drills, saying they undermine security and stability in the region.  
- The Taiwanese government stressed that China’s actions represent an open challenge to international law and the international order.  
- The presidential office reported that Taiwanese armed forces and security agencies have been fully briefed and are prepared.

## Hope for a peace solution in Ukraine

[Neue Zürcher Zeitung](https://www.nzz.ch/international/selenski-trifft-trump-hoffnung-auf-friedensloesung-in-der-ukraine-ld.1918201) • _Sunday, December 28 2025, 21:51 p.m._

The Ukrainian president pushed for a summit at Mar‑a‑Lago. Trump later claimed they were “much closer” to peace.

### Background and lead‑up
- In early 2025 **[Donald Trump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Trump)** kicked Ukrainian President **[Volodymyr Zelenskyy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volodymyr_Zelenskyy)** out of the Oval Office after a dispute.  
- At the end of 2025 Zelenskyy pressed for another meeting, this time at Trump’s residence **[Mar‑a‑Lago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mar-a-Lago)** in Palm Beach.  

### Meeting at Mar‑a‑Lago
- The first private meeting of the two heads of state took place on Sunday in the dining hall of Mar‑a‑Lago; media were allowed only brief statements.  
- Before the conversation Trump said:  
  - “We have everything needed for a deal.”  
  - Both presidents want “to reach an agreement” and the war must “finally end.”  
- Zelenskyy emphasized that **90 %** of the **20‑point peace plan** developed by Ukraine and the US was already ready.  

### Statements and positions of the participants
- **Trump**  
  - talked only about the “huge economic benefits” of peace for Ukraine.  
  - dismissed questions on security guarantees as “stupid,” but promised a “strong agreement” with heavy European involvement.  
  - ignored the recent Russian attack wave and referred to explosions in Russia that were not from the Congo.  
- **Zelenskyy**  
  - highlighted progress on security guarantees, citing a **95 %** agreement with the US.  
  - gave no concrete details on possible territorial concessions but mentioned the disputed area **[Donetsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donetsk_Oblast)** (about 25 % of the region, roughly the size of the canton of Bern).  
  - spoke of progress on a planned demilitarised zone.  

### Progress on the peace plan
- Both sides stressed they were “much closer to peace.”  
- Concrete results remained vague:  
  - Security guarantees for Ukraine were presented as almost finished.  
  - Discussions on Donetsk’s future and possible territorial compromises were described only as a “difficult topic,” without details.  
- The suggestion not to bomb the Russian‑occupied **[Zaporizhzhia Nuclear Power Plant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaporizhzhia_Nuclear_Power_Plant)** was called by Trump a “big step”; Putin was said to also help rebuild Ukraine, which Zelenskyy did not comment on.  

### Further diplomatic developments
- Before the Florida meeting Trump phoned **[Vladimir Putin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Putin)** and called the conversation “very good and very productive.”  
- Zelenskyy announced on X (formerly Twitter) that “by the turn of the year we could decide a lot,” seeing the diplomatic dynamism as especially active.  
- Trump emphasized that Zelenskyy could act only “if I approve it,” and made clear he would review the peace‑plan conditions.  

### Military situation (end of 2025)
- Along the 1 200 km front line the situation is static:  
  - Russian troops make only slow advances.  
  - **[Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia)** attacks on civilian targets have increased.  
  - Ukraine is striking energy and military facilities deep inside Russian hinterland more frequently.  

*The article shows that despite optimistic rhetoric at the Mar‑a‑Lago meeting, concrete progress on the peace process is still lacking and the battlefield remains tense.*

## Security guarantees for Ukraine in focus

[Neue Zürcher Zeitung](https://www.nzz.ch/international/selenski-trifft-trump-die-ukraine-draengt-auf-sicherheitsgarantien-und-luftabwehr-ld.1918188) • _Sunday, December 28 2025, 19:41 p.m._

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy wants to make it crystal clear to US President Donald Trump that capitulation and a dictated peace with Moscow are out of the question for Kyiv.

### Background of the meeting
- **Location & time**: On December 28 2025 the Ukrainian President **[Volodymyr Zelenskyy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volodymyr_Zelenskyy)** met US President **[Donald Trump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Trump)** at the **[Mar‑a‑Lago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mar-a-Lago)** estate (Florida).  
- **Lead‑up**:  
  - Zelenskyy wanted to present his 20‑point peace strategy, which was unveiled on Christmas Eve.  
  - Prior to the meeting Trump spoke with **[Vladimir Putin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Putin)**, calling the call “good and very productive.”  

### Core negotiation topics
- **Security guarantees**  
  - Focus on a strong security pact between the US and Ukraine.  
  - Zelenskyy demands that Western sanctions and political pressure on **[Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia)** not be lifted while attacks continue.  
- **Energy & nuclear power**  
  - Protection of the **[Zaporizhzhia Nuclear Power Plant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaporizhzhia_Nuclear_Power_Plant)** (Zaporizhzhia) as a critical point.  
- **Territorial issues**  
  - Discussion of possible land cessions, especially in the Donetsk area (part of the **[Donbas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donbas)**).  
  - Zelenskyy makes clear he will not accept concessions of Donbas parts not controlled by Russia, though he offers compromise proposals.  
- **Reconstruction & economy**  
  - Ukraine should draw “large economic benefits” from any territorial agreements.  
- **Air defence**  
  - Urgent need for additional air‑defence against drones, glide‑bombs and rockets threatening Ukraine’s energy supply.  

### Statements from the participants
- **Donald Trump**  
  - Emphasised the “too many dead” problem and his interest in a “strong security pact,” but gave no concrete timelines.  
  - Noted that “sensitive questions” about territories still need to be resolved.  
- **Volodymyr Zelenskyy**  
  - Reported progress in the dialogue, announcing US‑Ukrainian teams would meet next week to discuss details.  
  - Stressed his “red lines” and rejected fundamental territorial concessions.  
- **Vladimir Putin**  
  - Appeared in uniform, highlighted possible military integration of the Donbas and threatened to achieve goals “with armed force.”  
  - Accused Zelenskyy of having no interest in peace.  

### Outcomes and open questions
- **Short‑term outlook**:  
  - Both presidents signalled that a security agreement is “just around the corner,” yet concrete details are missing.  
- **Unresolved points**:  
  - Territorial compromises in the Donbas remain undefined.  
  - No binding commitments on additional air‑defence systems or concrete security guarantees from the US or European partners.  
- **Political implications**:  
  - Zelenskyy must justify any concessions to his public; his term was extended in 2024 under martial law.  
  - Trump faces domestic pressure to deliver quick successes.  

### Further context
- **International reactions**:  
  - EU allies are to be consulted after the meeting by Zelenskyy’s team.  
  - Russia holds fast to its maximalist demands and hopes Ukraine and its supporters become war‑weary.  
- **Next steps**:  
  - Planned talks between US and Ukrainian teams next week.  
  - Observation whether the US will keep a hard line toward Russia or pursue a rapid cease‑fire.  

> Relevance score: [6] – Discussion of security guarantees for Ukraine relates directly to the continuation of the conflict and the international security architecture, holding notable global importance though still contingent on diplomatic processes.
