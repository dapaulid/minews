## Ukraine and USA to Negotiate Peace Plan in Switzerland

[NZZ](https://www.nzz.ch/international/verhandlungen-in-genf-ukrainer-wollen-mit-amerikanern-und-europaeern-ueber-friedensplan-sprechen-ld.1913100) • _Saturday, November 22, 2025, 4:52 p.m._

President Trump expects Ukraine's agreement to his peace plan by Thursday. Kyiv hopes to negotiate better terms, also counting on help from Europeans.

### Negotiations in Geneva
- A US delegation meets Ukrainian representatives in [Geneva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geneva), Switzerland, on Sunday to discuss [Donald Trump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Trump)'s 28-point [peace plan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conference_for_Security_and_Co-operation_in_Europe).
  - US delegation led by Army Secretary and Ukraine envoy [Dan Driscoll](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dan_Driscoll), supplemented by Foreign Secretary [Marco Rubio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marco_Rubio) and special envoy [Steve Witkoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Witkoff).
  - Ukrainian delegation under Chief of Staff [Andriy Yermak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andriy_Yermak), including National Security Council head [Rustem Umerov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rustem_Umerov) and military intelligence chief [Kyrylo Budanov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyrylo_Budanov).
- [Switzerland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Switzerland) supports as host; [Bern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bern) in contact with all parties.
- Next steps: US then meets Russian negotiators, after which Trump is to sign an agreement with [Volodymyr Zelenskyy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volodymyr_Zelenskyy) that Russia agrees to but does not participate in.

### US Peace Plan and Criticism
- Plan provides for de facto cession of one-fifth of Ukrainian territory; Trump initially set deadline until Thursday but qualified it as extendable and not final offer.
- Criticism in the USA:
  - Republicans like Senator [Thom Tillis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thom_Tillis) warn that plan signals victory to [Putin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Putin).
  - Senators ([Angus King](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angus_King), [Mike Rounds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Rounds)) call plan a "Russian wishlist" according to Rubio's statement.
- Rubio contradicts on X: Plan US-authored, based on input from Russia and Ukraine.
- [J. D. Vance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J.D._Vance) criticizes Ukrainian "victory" fantasies despite more aid.

### Ukrainian Position under Pressure
- Zelenskyy does not openly reject plan to avoid accusation of refusing peace; appeals for national unity in face of critical decision.
- Ukraine confirms meeting; Zelenskyy warned of loss of dignity or loss of US partner.

### European Reaction
- Europeans (incl. [Friedrich Merz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friedrich_Merz), [Emmanuel Macron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emmanuel_Macron), [Keir Starmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keir_Starmer)) want to co-negotiate in Geneva; plan created without EU/[NATO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NATO) consultation.
- At [G20 summit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G20) in South Africa: Coordination meeting with Germany, France, Great Britain, Japan, Canada et al.; joint declaration sees plan as basis for work, rejects border changes by force, demands ceasefire only as opener.
- Goal: Give Zelenskyy backing; Merz warns of consequences for continent if Ukraine collapses.

### Corruption Scandal in Ukraine
- Zelenskyy weakened by scandal; demands for personnel changes, esp. at Yermak and Umerov (rumors of amnesty in plan denied).
- Delegation as signal: Zelenskyy sticks to confidants; processing pending due to war pressure.

> Relevance score: [8] - Diplomatic talks on Ukraine peace plan hosted in Switzerland; high potential impact on global conflict resolution and Swiss neutrality role.

## Fossil Fuels and Climate Goals in Focus

[Neue Zürcher Zeitung](https://www.nzz.ch/wissenschaft/weltklimagipfel-endet-ohne-beschluss-zu-exitplan-aus-fossilen-brennstoffen-eine-herbe-enttaeuschung-fuer-europaeische-diplomaten-ld.1912940) • _Saturday, November 22, 2025, 6:32 p.m._

Ten years after the Paris Agreement was adopted, governments still cannot bring themselves to phase out fossil fuels.

### End of COP30 without Decision on Fossil Fuels

- The [COP30](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_United_Nations_Climate_Change_Conference) in [Belém](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bel%C3%A9m) ended without a concrete commitment to phase out fossil fuels, despite dominant debates on the topic.
- Ten years after the [Paris Agreement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Agreement), the focus was for the first time on implementing goals, not new promises.
- [Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luiz_In%C3%A1cio_Lula_da_Silva) called for a roadmap to phase out fossil fuels and deforestation; in [Dubai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_United_Nations_Climate_Change_Conference) (two years ago) a transition was initiated for the first time, but consumption continues to rise.
- Decisions support implementation of climate plans and accelerate emission reductions, do not explicitly mention fossils; world heading toward 2.8 degrees of warming.

### Disappointment for Europe and Resistance from Emerging Countries

- Severe disappointment for European diplomats; alliance of over 80 countries (led by [Colombia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colombia)) demanded roadmap.
  - [Wopke Hoekstra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wopke_Hoekstra) (EU Commissioner) threatened blockade, accepted compromise as "bad deal".
- Strong resistance from emerging countries like [Saudi Arabia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saudi_Arabia), [China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China) and [Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia).
- Outlook:
  - [Colombia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colombia) and [Netherlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netherlands) plan conference in 2026.
  - Brazilian COP presidency (André Aranha Corrêa do Lago) working on roadmap.
- Tensions due to EU-[CBAM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EU_Carbon_Border_Adjustment_Mechanism), criticized by India and China as trade brake.

### Progress on Adaptation and Financing

- Focus on adaptation to climate change (floods, heat waves, droughts); agreement to triple financing by 2035, mainly by industrialized nations.
- Developing countries more satisfied:
  - [Sierra Leone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sierra_Leone): Significant steps (Jiwoh Abdulai).
  - [Kenya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenya): Demand for fulfillment of pledges (Ali Mohamed).
- Currently less than a third of climate financing goes to adaptation; EU wanted flexibility in exchange for stricter reduction obligations.

### Energy Transition and Brazilian Perspective

- Global energy transition underway with economic opportunities, but too slow for climate goals.
- [US](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) withdrawal major blow; COP30 emphasized laborious implementation under pressure (elections, fake news, frustration).
- Brazil used host role to show opportunities for emerging countries: New economy with growth and jobs through climate agenda.

> Relevance score: [7] - Highlights persistent global challenges in climate policy 10 years post-Paris Agreement; long-term economic and environmental significance.

## Thousands Demonstrate for Better Working Conditions

[Schweizer Radio und Fernsehen](https://www.srf.ch/news/schweiz/gesundheitsberufe-tausende-demonstrieren-in-bern-fuer-umsetzung-der-pflegeinitiative) • _Saturday, November 22, 2025, 5:16 p.m._

They demanded fair working conditions and secure healthcare.

### Demonstration in Bern
- Several thousand people demonstrated on Saturday afternoon on the [Bundesplatz, Bern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bundesplatz,_Bern) for rapid implementation of the [[Nursing Initiative (Switzerland)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nursing_Initiative_(Switzerland))].
- Organizers spoke of at least 5000 participants; unions like [Unia (trade union)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unia_(trade_union)), [VPOD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VPOD) and [Syna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syna) were represented.

### Demands and Criticism
- Demonstrators demanded fair working conditions and secure healthcare.
- Signs like "Clapping won't bring us back to work" criticized: Four years after the 2021 vote, conditions worthy of the demands are lacking; healthcare system deeper in crisis.
- Care workers expressed frustration with the "system", not bosses; [Berne SP National Councilor Flavia Wasserfallen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flavia_Wasserfallen) demanded binding requirements from the federal government.

### Alliance and Implementation Criticism
- Called by the [Alliance of Health Personnel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alliance_of_Health_Personnel), which includes the [Professional Association of Swiss Nursing Professionals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professional_Association_of_Swiss_Nursing_Professionals) and the [VSAO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VSAO).
- Criticism of the bill: Lack of secure staffing ratios and sufficient financing makes better conditions "hot air"; second stage (professional development) stuck in parliament.

### Background of the Nursing Initiative
- In November 2021, voters approved the initiative with 61% yes.
- Implementation in two stages:
  - First: Training offensive.
  - Second: Professional development of care workers (currently in parliament).

### Further Participants
- Director [Petra Volpe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petra_Volpe) joined; her film ["Heroine (2023)"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heroine_(2023_film)) highlights hospital staff and amplifies the voice of care workers.

> Relevance score: [6] - Swiss national demonstration for healthcare worker rights; moderate civic relevance to labor and health policy.
