## USA and Ukraine Agree on Peace Plan - What Does Moscow Say?

[GMX](https://www.gmx.ch/magazine/politik/russland-krieg-ukraine/usa-ukraine-friedensplan-moskau-41625814) • _Tuesday, November 25, 2025, 14:40_

Russian Foreign Minister Lavrov accuses Berlin and Paris of having missed their role as mediators in the Ukraine conflict.

### Agreement on US Peace Plan
- The [Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukraine) has agreed to the central points of the US peace plan, as [Rustem Umerov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rustem_Umerov), Secretary of the National Security Council, reports on [Facebook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Facebook).
- [Volodymyr Zelenskyy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volodymyr_Zelenskyy) plans a meeting with US President [Donald Trump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Trump) in November to clarify final details.
- US government source: Ukraine agrees, only "minor details" remain open; [Karoline Leavitt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karoline_Leavitt) (White House spokesperson) speaks of "delicate but solvable" points.
- The plan has been reduced from 28 to about 20 points; meeting in Abu Dhabi between US and Russian delegations.

### Russia's Stance
- [Sergey Lavrov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergey_Lavrov), Russian Foreign Minister, expects information from the [USA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) after consultations with Ukraine and Europe.
- Moscow sees the original plan as a basis for negotiations, warns against dilutions in favor of Kyiv.
- Lavrov rejects [Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany) and Europe as mediators, prefers [Belarus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belarus), [Turkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkey), [Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungary) or USA.
- Kremlin spokesperson [Dmitry Peskov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dmitry_Peskov): Europe to be involved later due to European security.

### European Coordination
- [Friedrich Merz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friedrich_Merz) ([Chancellor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chancellor_(Germany))) spoke on the phone with Zelenskyy, coordinating next steps including "Coalition of the Willing".
- [Keir Starmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keir_Starmer) ([Prime Minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Minister_of_the_United_Kingdom)): Ukraine's sovereignty, self-defense and freedom of decision.
- [Emmanuel Macron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emmanuel_Macron): Support with military security guarantees.
  - "Security forces" (including French soldiers) for training after ceasefire.
  - Strengthening the Ukrainian army through training and equipment for lasting peace.

### Military Incidents
- Russian air attack on [Kyiv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyiv): At least 7 dead, over 20 injured (drones, missiles, cruise missiles), reports Mayor [Vitali Klitschko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vitali_Klitschko).
- Ukrainian drone attack on southern Russia and [Crimea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crimea):
  - 3 dead in [Taganrog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taganrog) ([Rostov-on-Don Oblast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rostov_Oblast)).
  - 10 injured in Rostov, 6 in [Krasnodar Krai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krasnodar_Krai); heavy hits in [Novorossiysk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novorossiysk).
- Russian military: 249 drones intercepted, heaviest attack since the start of the war.

> Relevance score: [8] - US-Ukraine agreement on peace plan for Ukraine conflict; significant potential step toward resolution with global geopolitical impact.

## Warning of Millions of Additional HIV Infections

[GMX](https://www.gmx.ch/magazine/gesundheit/warnung-millionen-zusaetzlicher-hiv-infektionen-41625320) • _Tuesday, November 25, 2025, 14:00_

UN experts fear millions of additional HIV infections. The report shows what the loss of US funds means.

### UNAIDS Report on HIV Situation
- The new [UNAIDS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_United_Nations_Programme_on_HIV/AIDS) report, presented in Geneva on [World AIDS Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_AIDS_Day) (December 1), warns of up to 3.9 million additional [HIV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HIV) infections by 2030 due to cuts in international aid funds.
- Worldwide, there were about 1.3 million new infections with the [HIV virus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HIV) in 2024, which untreated causes [AIDS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AIDS); 630,000 deaths from AIDS-related causes; 40.8 million people live with HIV, over three-quarters have access to antiviral medications.

### Causes: Dependence on US Funds
- Since 2010, infection and death rates have declined, but international aid funds have been cut; USA temporarily covered three-quarters of global HIV funding.
- Early 2025, the US government froze payments, later resumed them; [UNAIDS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_United_Nations_Programme_on_HIV/AIDS) Executive Director [Winnie Byanyima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winnie_Byanyima) speaks of "devastating impacts," exacerbated by restrictions on rights for risk groups.

### Concrete Impacts
- Less access to preventive medications:
  - Uganda: -31% from end of 2024 to September 2025.
  - Burundi: -64% in the same period.
- Nigeria: Condom distribution more than halved (end of 2024 to March 2025).

### Positive Adjustments
- Over two dozen recipient countries are increasing domestic health budgets and HIV spending.

### Situation in Germany
- [Robert Koch Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Koch_Institute) estimates 2,300 new HIV infections in 2024, about 200 more than in 2023.

> Relevance score: [7] - UN warning of millions more HIV cases due to US funding cuts; major global public health risk with long-term societal impact.

## Federal Statistical Office - Swiss Median Wage at 7,024 Francs Gross

[Schweizer Radio und Fernsehen](https://www.srf.ch/news/schweiz/bundesamt-fuer-statistik-schweizer-medianlohn-liegt-bei-7024-franken-brutto) • _Tuesday, November 25, 2025, 12:35_

The wage gap between women and men continued to narrow according to the Federal Statistical Office.

### Median Wage Development
- The median wage of a full-time employee in [Switzerland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Switzerland) rose from 6,788 to 7,024 [francs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss_franc) gross (+3.5% between 2022 and 2024), according to initial results of the Swiss Wage Structure Survey 2024 by the [Federal Statistical Office](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Statistical_Office_(Switzerland)) ([FSO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Statistical_Office_(Switzerland))).
- The wage pyramid has hardly changed since 2008.
- Every tenth employee received a low wage (< 4,635 francs/month); the top-paid 10% earned > 12,526 francs.
- Nearly one-third (32.6%) received bonuses with an average annual value of 11,967 francs.

### Gender Differences
- The median gender pay gap narrowed to 8.4% (2024) from 9.5% (2022), 10.8% (2020) and 11.5% (2018).
- Differences are greater at higher hierarchical levels: Women in management positions earned 10,077 francs, men 11,715 francs (14% difference).

### Industry and Education Differences
- High wages above median: Research and development (9,139 francs), [pharmaceutical industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pharmaceutical_industry) (10,159), banks (10,723), tobacco industry (14,304).
- Medium wages: Metal production/processing (6,279), construction (6,616), aviation (7,134), wholesale (7,478), machine industry (7,632).
- Low wages: Retail (5,214), accommodation (4,715), gastronomy (4,744), personal services (4,496).
- Wage hierarchy by education:
  - University degree: 10,533 francs.
  - University of applied sciences degree: 9,288 francs.
  - [Federal Certificate of Proficiency (EFZ)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vocational_education_and_training_in_Switzerland#Federal_Certificate_of_Proficiency_(EFZ)): 6,390 francs.

### Regional Differences
- Nationwide: 7,024 francs; [Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canton_of_Z%C3%BCrich): 7,502 francs; [Ticino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canton_of_Ticino): 5,708 francs.
- Top-paid 10%: Zürich > 13,970 francs, Lake Geneva region 12,636, Eastern Switzerland 11,030, Ticino 10,012 francs.
- Differences persistent due to concentration of high-value industries and regional labor markets.

### Reactions
- Positive: Roland A. Müller ([Swiss Employers' Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss_Employers%27_Association)) sees stable picture despite Corona, wars; praises training, social partnership, social mobility.
- Critical: Daniel Lampart ([Swiss Trade Union Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss_Trade_Union_Federation)) criticizes stagnating real wages despite productivity gains and skilled labor shortages; demands tougher action.

> Relevance score: [7] - Official Swiss median wage data and narrowing gender gap; key national economic indicator affecting civic understanding.

## Novartis Cuts 550 Jobs in Stein AG

[Schweizer Radio und Fernsehen](https://www.srf.ch/news/schweiz/stellenabbau-bei-pharmakonzern-novartis-streicht-550-stellen-in-stein-ag) • _Tuesday, November 25, 2025, 08:58_

Novartis is ceasing tablet and capsule production at its plant in Stein near Basel.

### Job Cuts in Stein AG
- The pharmaceutical company [Novartis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novartis) plans to cut around 550 permanent positions at the [Stein AG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novartis#Sites) site in the [Canton of Aargau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canton_of_Aargau) by the end of 2027.
- Cessation of tablet and capsule production as well as packaging of sterile medicines.
- All steps subject to consultation; Novartis offers support measures and an extended social plan.

### Automation and Remaining Production in Stein
- Increase in automation to secure competitive production in Switzerland through focus on innovative manufacturing technologies.
- Remaining: Production of sterile dosage forms and personalized cell therapies.
- Increased automation for pre-filled syringes and injection solutions with investment of 26 million dollars (21 million francs).

### Investments in Schweizerhalle BL
- Investment of 80 million dollars (65 million francs) in production at the [Schweizerhalle BL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schweizerhalle) site.
- Creation of around 80 new full-time positions by end of 2028.
- Expansion of production of si[RNA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SiRNA) molecules as building blocks for cardiovascular, kidney and metabolic diseases.
- Quote from Steffen Lang, President Operations: "With the planned adjustments, we are developing both sites […] as centers of excellence for innovative production."

### Background and Reactions
- No connection to US investments for the US market (April announcement).
- SRF specialist editor [Jan Baumann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SRG_SSR): Typical step for global pharma companies – cost savings in labor-intensive production at high-wage locations; strategic adjustment also in context of [Donald Trump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Trump)'s demands, but no direct connection.
- Union [Unia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unia_(trade_union)) demands review of alternatives, extension of consultation process and use of employee suggestions; criticism of recent Novartis assurances that no jobs were at risk.

> Relevance score: [7] - 550 job cuts at Novartis site in Switzerland; significant for national pharma sector and local economy.

## 5000 Tons Annually - Medications: Three Billion Francs End Up in Waste

[Schweizer Radio und Fernsehen](https://www.srf.ch/sendungen/kassensturz-espresso/kassensturz/5000-tonnen-jaehrlich-medikamente-drei-milliarden-franken-landen-im-abfall) • _Tuesday, November 25, 2025, 14:54_

In Switzerland, medications worth 3 billion francs end up in waste annually.

### Waste Volume and Costs
- Annually, around **[5,000 tons of medications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_waste)** are disposed of in Switzerland, with a value of at least **[3 billion francs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss_franc)**.
- The figures come from hospitals, nursing homes and pharmacies; private households are not included.
- Medications are considered special waste and must be returned since 2001 (pharmacies, drugstores, special waste sites, family doctors); return partly free, partly fee-based.

### Solutions
- Federal Council plans individual dispensing of **[antibiotics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antibiotic)**; pilot project in **[Canton of Neuchâtel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canton_of_Neuch%C3%A2tel)** (2020) brought no major savings.
- Céline Gardiole ([FOPH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Office_of_Public_Health)) emphasizes focus on **[antibiotic resistance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antimicrobial_resistance)**, not cost reduction; costs could even rise.

### Criticism of Package Sizes
- Pascal Bonnabry (Head of Hospital Pharmacy **[Geneva University Hospitals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geneva_University_Hospitals)**) demands smaller, therapy-adapted packages (e.g., 21 tablets instead of 20 for 7-day treatment).
- Industry ([Interpharma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interpharma), Michèle Sierro) rejects criticism: Package sizes set by [FOPH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Office_of_Public_Health) upon inclusion in specialties list.
  - FOPH reviews appropriateness every 3 years; can refuse inclusion or demand adjustment.
  - Different indications/dosages make perfect packages impossible.

### Effectiveness of Expired Medications
- RTS test ([University of Lausanne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Lausanne)): 7 out of 8 expired preparations (e.g., Dafalgan, Ibuprofen, Sortis, Buscopan) still effective (>90% active ingredient).
- Expert Marc Augsburger advises against intake, advocates review of longer shelf-life data.
- Interpharma: Data scientific, guarantee 100% stability; companies prefer long data.

### Political Initiative
- **[SP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_Democratic_Party_of_Switzerland)** National Councilor **[Brigitte Crottaz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brigitte_Crottaz)** demands: Doctors/patients/health insurers can complain about unsuitable packages; industry reimburses costs.
- FOPH plans from 2026 (ordinance revision): Consider waste in price setting.

> Relevance score: [6] - Annual waste of 3B CHF in meds in Switzerland; highlights economic and environmental inefficiency with civic relevance.

## Egg Production: No More Chicks to Be Killed from 2026

[Schweizer Radio und Fernsehen](https://www.srf.ch/sendungen/kassensturz-espresso/espresso/eierproduktion-in-der-schweiz-an-neujahr-ist-es-vorbei-mit-dem-kuekentoeten) • _Tuesday, November 25, 2025, 08:02_

Conventional and organic egg producers reach their goal: As of January 1, the large-scale chick culling ends.

### Background and Promise
- Dark side of egg production: Male chicks were killed with CO₂ immediately after hatching from conventional and organic laying hens, as they do not lay eggs and grow too slowly for meat production.
- Swiss egg producers promised to end chick culling as of January 1, 2026.
- Confirmation: [Gallosuisse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gallosuisse) (conventional) and [Bio Suisse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bio_Suisse) state that the switch is already practically complete.

### Impact on Consumers
- Egg price rises by almost two cents due to higher costs for [MRI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnetic_resonance_imaging) scans or rearing brother cocks.
- Call: Buy products from brother cock meat and [dual-purpose hens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dual-purpose_chicken); brother cock meat has a different structure and is not as white as normal chicken.

### Implementation at Bio Suisse
- Brother cocks reared, [dual-purpose hens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dual-purpose_chicken) kept (for eggs and meat); laying hens usable as soup hens.
- Project manager Adrian Schlageter: Goal reached as of January 1, 2026, industry used four-year deadline.
- Challenges:
  - More space needed (for 600,000 organic laying hens, similarly many brother cocks).
  - Solution: Laying hens longer in laying barns, fewer hens, space free in rearing barns.
- Meat sales: Partners [Coop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coop_(Switzerland)) and [Migros](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Migros), processed into charcuterie like hot dogs or cold cuts.

### Implementation at Gallosuisse
- Sex determination in egg after 12 days using MRI (similar to hospital tube).
- Male eggs sorted out, only female hatched; possible for all eggs since mid-March (with functional system).
- President Daniel Würgler: Very much on track.
- Challenges:
  - Accuracy and cost-effectiveness of MRI system (no female chicks sorted out).
  - Error rate 2–5%: Male chicks sorted later, killed with CO₂, used as feed (e.g., zoos).
  - More space/time, extended working hours, staff training.
  - Spring: Major analysis including costs; monitoring better technologies.

### Conclusion
- Gallosuisse and Bio Suisse see switch as important step for the industry; process continues.

> Relevance score: [6] - End to male chick killing in Swiss egg production; ethical advance in agriculture with national civic relevance.
